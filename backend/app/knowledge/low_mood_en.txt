[Validation]
At times people may feel low in energy, unmotivated, or less interested in things they usually enjoy. These feelings can happen when someone has been under stress, dealing with setbacks, or feeling emotionally tired.

---

[Technique: Small Step Activation]
When energy feels low, starting with a very small action can help. It might be something simple like standing up, taking a short walk, or doing one small task. Small steps can sometimes help the mind and body regain momentum.

---

[Technique: Self-Compassion Pause]
Try pausing for a moment and noticing how you are speaking to yourself. If your thoughts are harsh or critical, gently replace them with a kinder statement, such as “I’m going through a difficult moment, and that’s okay.”

---

[Technique: Activity Recall]
Think about one activity that used to feel even slightly enjoyable or calming. It doesn’t have to be something big. Reconnecting with a familiar activity for a few minutes can sometimes lift the mood a little.

---

[Reflective Prompt]
What has your energy or motivation been like lately? If you feel comfortable, you can share what the past few days have been like for you.

---

[Escalation Note]
If low mood continues for a long time, becomes stronger, or begins to interfere with daily life, reaching out to a trusted person or a mental health professional may provide helpful support.
