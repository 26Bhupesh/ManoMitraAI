[Validation]
Sometimes people find it difficult to fall asleep or stay asleep, especially when the mind is busy or the day has been stressful. Thoughts may keep running even when the body feels tired.

---

[Technique: Gentle Breathing]
Before trying to sleep, take a few slow breaths. Breathing in calmly through the nose and breathing out slowly can help the body begin to relax.

---

[Technique: Wind-Down Routine]
A short calming routine before bed may help the mind slow down. This could include dimming lights, listening to quiet music, or doing something relaxing away from screens.

---

[Technique: Thought Parking]
If thoughts keep repeating, try briefly noting them down on paper or in your phone. Writing them down can sometimes help the mind let go of them for the night.

---

[Reflective Prompt]
Has something in particular been keeping your mind active when you try to sleep?

---

[Escalation Note]
If sleep difficulties continue for a long time or begin affecting your daily life, it may help to speak with a trusted person or a mental health professional.
