[Validation]
People sometimes experience anger or irritation when they feel treated unfairly, misunderstood, or under pressure. Strong emotions can cause the body to feel tense and thoughts to move quickly.

---

[Technique: Pause and Breathe]
If emotions feel intense, try pausing for a moment before reacting. Take a slow breath in and out a few times. A short pause can give your mind space to settle before deciding what to do next.

---

[Technique: Physical Release]
Anger can build physical tension in the body. Gentle movement such as walking, stretching, or shaking out your hands can help release some of that energy.

---

[Technique: Perspective Check]
Sometimes it can help to step back and ask, “What exactly is upsetting me right now?” Naming the specific issue can make it easier to respond calmly instead of reacting immediately.

---

[Reflective Prompt]
Would you like to share what situation or thought seems to be triggering this feeling?

---

[Escalation Note]
If anger feels overwhelming, happens very frequently, or begins affecting relationships or daily life, speaking with a trusted person or a mental health professional may provide helpful support.
