[Validation]
Sometimes people simply want a space to talk, reflect, or sort through their thoughts. Sharing what is on your mind can sometimes help bring a little clarity.

---

[Technique: Gentle Pause]
Taking a brief moment to slow down can help organize thoughts. A few calm breaths or a short pause may make it easier to notice what you are feeling or thinking.

---

[Technique: Thought Sorting]
If many thoughts are coming at once, it may help to focus on one idea at a time. Describing a single concern or situation can make conversations clearer.

---

[Technique: Present Moment Noticing]
Bringing attention to something simple around you, such as a sound, a sensation, or your breathing, can sometimes help the mind settle before continuing a conversation.

---

[Reflective Prompt]
What has been on your mind recently?

---

[Escalation Note]
If you ever feel overwhelmed or feel that you need more support than this conversation can provide, reaching out to a trusted person or a mental health professional may be helpful.
