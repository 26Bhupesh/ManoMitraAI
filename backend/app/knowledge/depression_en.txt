[CONTEXT]
Depression can make everyday activities feel heavy and exhausting. It may show up as low mood, loss of interest, tiredness, difficulty concentrating, or feeling disconnected from others.

[VALIDATION]
Feeling this way does not mean you are weak or failing. Many people experience depression at different points in life, especially during prolonged stress or loss.

[TECHNIQUE]
Start with very small actions. Choose one simple task, such as drinking a glass of water or opening a window, and focus only on that.

[GUIDANCE]
You do not need to feel motivated first. Often, action comes before motivation, not the other way around.

[REFLECTION]
Was there even a slight shift in how you felt after doing something small?
---

[CONTEXT]
Depression can affect how you think, making negative thoughts feel constant and convincing.

[VALIDATION]
These thoughts feel real, but they are symptoms of depression, not facts about who you are or what the future holds.

[TECHNIQUE]
Try writing down one negative thought and then writing one alternative explanation, even if it feels hard to believe at first.

[GUIDANCE]
The goal is not to force positivity, but to gently question whether there could be more than one perspective.

[REFLECTION]
What was it like to see the thought written down instead of only in your mind?
---

[CONTEXT]
Low energy is common in depression, and even rest may not feel refreshing.

[VALIDATION]
Feeling tired all the time does not mean you are lazy. Your mind and body are under strain.

[TECHNIQUE]
Consider pacing your day. Alternate short periods of activity with rest, rather than pushing yourself all at once.

[GUIDANCE]
Rest is not a failure. It is part of taking care of yourself.

[REFLECTION]
What helps you feel even slightly less drained during the day?
---

[CONTEXT]
Depression can create a sense of isolation, even when others are around.

[VALIDATION]
Feeling alone does not mean you truly are alone. Depression can make connection feel distant or difficult.

[TECHNIQUE]
Reach out in a low-pressure way. This could be sending a short message or sitting near someone, without needing a deep conversation.

[GUIDANCE]
Connection does not have to be intense to matter.

[REFLECTION]
Is there one person or place that feels a little safer or easier to connect with?
